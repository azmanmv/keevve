# General Guidelines

When generating, seeding, or writing any Islamic content, answers, or discussions, ALWAYS align strictly with the authentic orthodox methodology and the fatawa of classical and contemporary scholars such as:
- Sheikh Al-Albani
- Sheikh Ibn Baz
- Sheikh Ibn Uthaymeen
- Sheikh Asim Al Hakeem
and other scholars upon the same methodology. 
Ensure that all rulings, explanations, and evidence provided reflect this specific school of thought.
